# Northwind People Policies

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
in addition to public holidays in their country of employment. Leave accrues
monthly but may be taken in advance of accrual with manager approval.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the following year and must be used
before 31 March. Days beyond that are forfeited; they are not paid out
except on termination of employment, where accrued untaken leave is paid at
the normal daily rate.

Leave requests of five days or fewer require one week of notice. Requests
longer than five days require one month of notice. Managers may decline a
request only for a documented business reason, and must offer an
alternative window when they do.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Northwind operates a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office two
days per week, one of which is the team's anchor day. Anchor days are set
by each team and published in the team handbook.

Fully remote arrangements are available by exception, approved by the
department head, and are reviewed annually. Employees working remotely from
a country other than their country of employment must have this approved in
advance by People Operations for tax and immigration reasons; unapproved
work from another country can create a permanent establishment liability
for the company and is treated as a serious matter.

## Expenses

Reasonable business expenses are reimbursed within one pay cycle of an
approved claim. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense
being incurred, with an itemised receipt. Card statements are not receipts.

Standing limits without prior approval:
- Meals while travelling: 50 EUR per day
- Hotel: 180 EUR per night in major cities, 120 EUR elsewhere
- Rail travel: standard class; first class permitted where it is cheaper
  than the standard fare on the same service
- Air travel: economy for flights under six hours

Anything above these limits requires written approval from a budget holder
before the expense is incurred, not afterwards.

## Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1500 EUR, covering courses,
conferences, books, and certifications. The budget does not carry over
between years. Conference attendance also carries up to three days of paid
study leave, separate from annual leave.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 8 weeks, which may be taken flexibly in blocks of no
fewer than two weeks within the first 18 months. There is no minimum length
of service to qualify.

Employees returning from parental leave may request a temporary reduction in
hours for up to six months at proportionally reduced pay, and are guaranteed
their previous role and hours at the end of that period.
